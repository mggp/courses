## Notable threads for the later breakdown
- **Technology enables style** — Cascading author/reader/device balance (CSS), then Flash's canvas+timeline, then media queries (responsive), then WOFF/web fonts.
- **Style vs. platform authority** — Apple (HIG, iOS 7), Google (Material), Microsoft (Fluent/Acrylic) each codify a visual language; acrylic precedes/overlaps glassmorphism.
- **Docu trends are informal movements** — brutalism, neumorphism, glassmorphism, and Y2K have naming/origin moments rather than formal specs; they resist single definitions (brutalism is "a reaction," notes the study).\n- **Rehabilitations / fallacies** — neumorphism was briefly mainstream (Plyuto, 2020), boosted by macOS Big Sur, then rejected by 2021 chiefly on accessibility/low-contrast grounds — a reminder that a style's arc is often shaped by usability criticism, not only aesthetics.
- **Rebellion against homogenization** — Web 2.0 backlash (Snarkmarket), Deville's reaction to "neutralised interfaces," and the post-Flash move toward usability/minimalism all recur.
- **Continuity of traits** — translucency, gradients, gloss, metallic/pearlescent, pixelated type, rounded shapes reappear across Y2K, iOS 7, Material, Fluent, glassmorphism and neumorphism, so the breakdown can trace traits rather than only labels.
