# Verifying a responsive transition before revising it

The learner can assign minimum useful widths to adjacent regions, stress the composition beyond its normal responsive behavior, and distinguish an artificial failure from a defect users can encounter. They also accept “no revision” when the transition happens before failure and the narrow composition preserves access to the primary task.

## Evidence

For a sidebar and chat composition, the learner estimated minimum widths of 187px and 320px. Icon overflow occurred only after manually breaking apart the responsive behavior. In normal use, the sidebar becomes a top navigation bar before overflow and scrolls away, leaving the chat as the primary region.
