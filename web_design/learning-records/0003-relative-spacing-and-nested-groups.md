# Comparing spacing by relationship

The learner can measure adjacent gaps and explain how similar distances make separate controls read as one group. Future practice should refine the distinction between a semantic group and a relationship inside a nested group: label and field form a control unit, the control unit and action form the form, and the form joins the instruction to form the task.

## Evidence

On the code-entry screen, the learner identified the tight label-to-field relationship, noticed that the nearly equal field-to-button gap collapses the controls into one undifferentiated unit, and proposed changing spacing without changing type or color.
