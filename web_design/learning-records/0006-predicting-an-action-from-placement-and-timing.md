# Predicting an action from placement and timing

The learner can infer a control's apparent role from where and when it appears, then compare that prediction with its actual behavior. Future interaction-pattern lessons can assume they inspect the whole presentation of a control, not only its label or component type; the next refinement is to distinguish the diagnosed mismatch from the chosen repair.

## Evidence

In a question card, the learner identified that a newly appearing button directly below selected answers can be mistaken for the submit action, even though submission lives in a fixed page-wide bar and the new button only clears the selection. They proposed stabilizing both actions by keeping them visible and disabling them when no answer is selected.
