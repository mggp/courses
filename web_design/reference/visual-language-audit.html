Visual-language audit sheet

Use this to turn “I want this page to feel…” into observable, repeatable rules.

AxisDescribe the ruleStress test
TypeFamily category, contrast, weights, scale, and spacing.Would a heading and a button label still look as if they belong together?
GeometryCorner radius, border weight, icons, and shapes.Could you predict the treatment of a new input or notice from the existing controls?
Surface/colorBackground character, layering, palette restraint, and accent use.Does color add meaning and emphasis without becoming the only signal?
DensityReading width, whitespace, grouping, and amount of simultaneous content.Does the density support the page’s task and hierarchy?
Imagery/motionImage subject, crop, treatment, and motion character.Would removing it harm comprehension, or merely remove decoration?

Brief template

“For [audience and task], use [type rule], [geometry rule], and [surface/color rule]. Preserve [attention order and interaction pattern]. Do not use [accessibility or hierarchy failure].”

Prefer observable decisions over labels such as “modern,” “premium,” or “brutalist.” A label may help search for references; it cannot do the work of a rule.
