Quick reference

Audit visual rhythm

Compare the same relationship across repeated structures. Keep a variation only when it communicates a variation in meaning.

1. Find repetition

Choose at least three structures that perform the same job: cards, rows, sections, controls, or text blocks.

2. Name one relationship

Compare like with like. Measure title to summary, label to field, item to item, or another named relationship.

3. Inspect more than gaps

Rhythm can repeat through spacing, line height, size, alignment, shape, and recurring component structure.

4. Explain exceptions

Ask what changed in the content. A new section or featured item can justify a break. An unchanged role cannot.

5. Repair one variable

Change only the unsupported measurement, then scan again. Do not redesign the whole component to fix one broken pattern.

6. Stress text spacing

Increase line, paragraph, word, and letter spacing. The pattern may expand, but content must not clip, overlap, or disappear.

A critique sentence

“These components perform the same job, but their title-to-summary gaps vary without a change in meaning. Assign that relationship one spacing token and reserve exceptions for real structural changes.”
