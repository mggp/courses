# Phase 4 — Influence

> Deepen the influence-without-authority strand. Write a Tier 2 ADR focused on stakeholder argumentation and cost/risk framing.

## Duration

**4 weeks / ~45 hours** (11h/wk average)

## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 15 — Influence Theory
- Read *Influence Without Authority* (Cohen & Bradford) — full book
- Read Phil Calcado's "How to Write an RFC" article
- Read Amazon 6-pager / PR-FAQ process examples
- **Exercise**: Identify a stakeholder in your org who disagrees with an architecture decision. Write down their likely objections. What currency (influence) can you offer them?
- **Hours**: 12

### Week 16 — Stakeholder Communication
- Read *Articulating Design Decisions* (Greever) selected chapters on communicating up and across
- Write a mock RFC for a decision you want to make (can be aspirational)
- **Exercise**: Take one technical concept from Phase 2 or 3 and explain it in 3 different ways: (a) your engineering peer, (b) your product manager, (c) a CTO
- **Hours**: 12

### Week 17 — Cost/Risk Framing
- Read Well-Architected Cost Pillar whitepaper
- Learn basic cost modeling: compute vs storage vs data transfer cost estimation
- Start writing Tier 2 influencer ADR (Context + Constraints + Options + cost estimates)
- **Exercise**: Estimate the monthly cost of one service in your system. Break down by compute, storage, data transfer.
- **Hours**: 11 (4h reading + 4h cost exercise + 3h ADR start)

### Week 18 — Complete ADR + AI Challenge
- Finish Tier 2 influencer ADR: add Decision + Rationale + stakeholder argumentation
- **AI session (2h)**: Stakeholder argumentation — "How would you defend this to a non-technical CTO?" "Who disagrees and how do you handle it?"
- Write multi-audience summaries (engineering / PM / CTO versions)
- **Exercise**: Write a mock Slack message to a non-technical stakeholder explaining your decision in 5 sentences
- **Hours**: 10 (6h ADR + 2h AI + 2h flex)

## Lead Mode Deliverable
1 Tier 2 ADR that explicitly addresses stakeholder concerns and includes cost/risk analysis.

## Contribute Mode Deliverable
The same decision documented as a case study with a "stakeholder landscape" section — who cared, what their concerns were, how they were addressed.

## Influence Component
Stakeholder persuasion — this is the depth phase for the influence strand. ADR addresses "who cares about this decision and why."

## Checkpoint Gate
Authored a Tier 2 ADR with stakeholder argumentation. Can frame a decision in cost/risk terms.

## Deferral Markers
- Drop *Influence Without Authority* book → use article summaries only (−5h)
- Drop cost modeling exercise → keep conceptual (−4h)
- Skip multi-audience summaries → keep ADR only (−4h)
- Final fallback: Week 18 flex absorbs up to 2h
