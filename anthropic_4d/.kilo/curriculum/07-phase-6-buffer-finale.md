# Phase 6 — Buffer Finale

> Absorb overruns from phases 1–5. Complete any unfinished artifact. Polish the case study. Close the 6-month track.

## Duration

**2 weeks / ~26 hours** (13h/wk average)

## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 23 — Overrun Absorption
- Review per-phase deferral markers from phases 1–5
- Identify which deferred items are highest priority to complete
- Complete any unfinished ADR or case study sections
- **Exercise**: Triage your deferral backlog — what must be done, what can stay deferred, what can be dropped
- **Hours**: 14

### Week 24 — Retrospective + Polish
- Write a 2-page learning narrative: "What I learned as an architect in 6 months" — suitable for a portfolio or performance review
- Polish all artifacts for presentation quality
- Triage the ancillary backlog for post-6-month extension
- **Exercise**: Read your Phase 1 practice ADRs. How would you write them differently now?
- **Hours**: 12 (6h narrative + 6h polish/triage)

## Lead Mode Deliverable
Final polished Tier 3 ADR, with all revisions applied and Defense Questionnaire complete.

## Contribute Mode Deliverable
Final polished case study, portfolio-ready.

## Influence Component
Retrospective communication — produce a learning narrative that demonstrates growth and self-awareness.

## Checkpoint Gate
All incomplete artifacts from phases 1–5 are complete. Case study and/or capstone ADR is polished and ready to show a hiring manager.

## Deferral Markers
The whole phase is a deferral sink. If phases 1–5 completed on time, this phase shrinks to ~20h and you close early.

---

## End of 6-Month Track

After this phase:
1. Your portfolio contains: a Tier 3 ADR + case study + learning narrative
2. The ancillary backlog is prioritized for post-6-month extension
3. You have written 5+ ADRs across Tier 0–3
4. You have practiced influence skills across 6 dimensions
5. You have a documented architecture vocabulary (NFRs, trade-off analysis, patterns) to reference in interviews
