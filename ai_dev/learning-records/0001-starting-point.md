**Evidence**: Direct disclosure in the opening session, plus the working agent (proof of at least one LLM-pipeline build).

**Implications**: Lessons must open with *building*, not explaining concepts he already knows — Lesson 1 (minimal RAG from scratch) targets exactly this gap. Compliance constraint means all practice is personal/after-hours; prefer local and low-cost setups. The language-learning app and fintech use cases both ground in RAG, so it is the right spine for the near-term curriculum.
