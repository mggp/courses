# Mission: Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate certification

## Why
Pass the current Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate exam and be able to apply the tested concepts to realistic Databricks agent and RAG scenarios.

## Success looks like
- Correctly solve scenario questions tied to every objective in the March 18, 2026 exam guide.
- Explain why the correct choice fits and why the distractors fail.
- Use current Databricks product names and documented behavior.

## Constraints
- Teach only objectives present in `references/Gen-AI-Guide.pdf`.
- Use official Databricks documentation and Databricks Academy as knowledge sources.
- State uncertainty instead of filling documentation gaps from memory.
- Build retention through short lessons and retrieval practice.

## Out of scope
- Third-party tutorials, blogs, and forum posts.
- Databricks features unrelated to an objective in the exam guide.
- Unverified predictions about exam questions or product behavior.
