# Design Applications quiz result and question-quality feedback

The learner answered all 10 Design Applications practice questions correctly. This supports moving beyond introductory recognition questions toward scenario-based questions that require judgment.

Future practice questions should not reveal the answer through their wording or a stated sequence of events. In particular, avoid asking for an obvious next step when the scenario already specifies the required order. Instead, present realistic business constraints and plausible alternatives that require the learner to select the appropriate model task, chain component, tool sequence, Agent Bricks capability, or input-output contract.

The learner answered `1B, 2C, 3A, 4A, 5B, 6A, 7B, 8C, 9B, 10A`, all correctly, and specifically identified questions 5, 9, and 10 as trivial because their wording embedded the answer.
